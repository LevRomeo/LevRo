# Source and verification policy

Use two independent source classes for material conclusions.

## Rankings

- Tonghuashun: use the verifiable Top30 and retain rank/date/retrieval time.
- Eastmoney: use the verifiable Top100 and retain rank/date/retrieval time.
- Formal universe: previous trading-day union.
- Priority eligibility: verified intersection only.
- Current-day lists: observation only.

## Quotes and bars

- Cross-check timestamp, current price, previous close, high/low, volume, and bar adjustment method.
- Label partial-day moving averages as dynamic/provisional.
- Do not compare partial-day volume directly with full-day averages without time adjustment.

## Announcements and catalysts

- Prefer exchange, CNINFO, company, regulator, or government sources.
- Use media or data vendors as a second source, not the sole source for material claims.
- Separate announced investment from orders, revenue, profit, and cash collection.
- State when data is inaccessible, stale, conflicting, or current-only.

## Privacy

- Public market indices and ranking lists are non-personal market data.
- A user's position list, entry prices, and private watchlist are personal context.
- Send a position code to a quote endpoint only when that endpoint/code/purpose is authorized in state.
